The game

You are the tanker. Everyone else in the strait wants you on the bottom.

Run east

Load six drums on the way through. Every one aboard makes her heavier and slower to turn. There is always a clean line through the mines — it is on your chart, top right.

Fight through

A deck gun that aims itself — Space to fire, and it jams if you hold it. Shore batteries shell you at the narrows; the red ring is where it lands. Attack craft make passes. Jets strafe anything in open water. Smoke on E breaks a lock.

Turn and come home

Clearing the eastern gate is the halfway point. The run back west is loaded, slower, and they know you are coming. Score is drums, kills, mines cleared, batteries silenced, time, and hull.

Play in the browser Keyboard on desktop, touch controls on a phone. No wallet needed to play.

Plainly
